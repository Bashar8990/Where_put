Fix undo from ItemDetailsPage: stay on page during undo window instead of navigating away

The delete flow was navigating to home immediately, but the undo toast
callback called load() on the unmounted ItemDetailsPage, so the restored
item never appeared in HomePage. Now:
- Show a "deleted" state on the details page during the 7s undo window
- Undo callback safely calls load() on the still-mounted component
- Navigate to home only after the undo window expires
